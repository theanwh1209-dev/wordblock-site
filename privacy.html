WordBlock privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

WordBlock is an Android app that asks an English vocabulary question before apps you choose can open. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: WordBlock does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, and no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Information that stays on your phone

These are saved only on your device, to make the app work:

  • your word progress, streak, daily goal and settings;
  • word lists you import, and any meanings you edit;
  • the list of apps you choose to block;
  • which app is on screen, read with the Usage access permission you grant, used only at that moment to decide whether to show a quiz. It is not recorded or kept as a history.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. WordBlock cannot see what you do inside other apps.

Permissions

  • Usage access: to notice when a blocked app opens.
  • Display over other apps: to show the quiz on top of it.
  • Notifications: for the ongoing notification Android shows while apps are guarded.
  • Run at startup and in the background: to keep the guard running and restart it after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: only for purchases through Google Play. Word lists and progress are never uploaded.

Importing word lists

When you import a file, WordBlock reads it on your phone through Android's file picker. Missing meanings come from a dictionary built into the app, so no words are sent anywhere. If you use an AI chat service to make a list, that happens in that service's own app, under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

WordBlock Premium is bought through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. WordBlock only receives confirmation of what was bought, to unlock Premium on your phone.

Pronunciation

Word sounds use your phone's own text-to-speech engine.

Backups

If you have turned on Android backup, Android may back up the app's data to your Google account, under Google's privacy policy. You can switch this off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling WordBlock, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes everything it saved. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete.

Children

WordBlock is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email the developer at the address shown on WordBlock's Google Play page.

WordBlock 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 28 日

WordBlock 是一款安卓应用，在你选择的应用打开之前先出一道英语单词题。本政策说明应用如何处理信息。简单来说：WordBlock 不收集、不在服务器上存储、不出售也不分享任何个人数据。无需注册账号，没有广告、数据分析或追踪。

只保存在你手机上的信息

以下信息只保存在你的设备上，用于让应用正常运行：

  • 你的单词进度、连续天数、每日目标和设置；
  • 你导入的词表，以及你修改过的释义；
  • 你选择要锁住的应用列表；
  • 当前在屏幕上的是哪个应用（通过你授予的“使用情况访问权限”读取），仅在当下用于判断是否需要弹出测验，不会被记录或保存为历史。

这些信息都不会发送给我们或任何其他人。WordBlock 无法看到你在其他应用里做什么。

权限

  • 使用情况访问权限：用于发现被锁的应用何时打开。
  • 显示在其他应用上层：用于在其上方显示测验。
  • 通知：守护运行时，安卓会显示一条常驻通知。
  • 开机启动和后台运行：保持守护运行，并在手机重启后自动恢复。
  • 网络：仅用于通过 Google Play 购买。词表和学习进度绝不会上传。

导入词表

导入文件时，WordBlock 通过安卓的文件选择器在你的手机上读取文件。缺少的释义来自应用内置的词典，不会把任何单词发送出去。如果你使用 AI 聊天服务来制作词表，那是在该服务自己的应用里进行，受其隐私政策约束。

购买

WordBlock 高级版通过 Google Play 购买。付款信息由 Google 按照其隐私政策处理。WordBlock 只会收到购买确认，用于在你的手机上解锁高级版。

发音

单词发音使用你手机自带的文字转语音引擎。

备份

如果你开启了安卓备份，安卓可能会按照 Google 的隐私政策将应用数据备份到你的 Google 账号。你可以在手机设置中关闭。

删除数据

卸载 WordBlock，或在安卓设置中清除其存储空间，就会删除它保存的所有内容。其他地方没有任何需要删除的数据。

儿童

WordBlock 并非为 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不会有意收集任何人的信息。

变更

如果本政策有变更，新版本会发布在此页面并更新日期。

联系方式

如对本政策有疑问，请发送邮件至 WordBlock 在 Google Play 页面上显示的开发者邮箱。
