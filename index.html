Questions

常见问题

Does WordBlock see what I do in other apps?

No. It only checks which app is on screen, so it knows when to show a quiz. It never sees what you do inside an app, and nothing leaves your phone.

WordBlock 能看到我在其他应用里做什么吗？

不能。它只知道屏幕上是哪个应用，以便在需要时弹出测验。它看不到你在应用里的任何内容，也不会上传任何数据。

Can I still use a blocked app when I need it?

Yes. Answer the quiz and it opens. You can also give yourself free minutes after a correct answer, or switch the guard off at any time.

需要用被锁的应用时还能打开吗？

可以。答完测验就能打开。你也可以设置答对后的自由时间，或随时关闭守护。

Is it a security tool?

No, it's a nudge. A blocked app still opens if the guard is switched off or WordBlock is uninstalled. The parent / partner PIN makes switching it off a deliberate choice.

它是安全防护工具吗？

不是，它是一个提醒。关闭守护或卸载 WordBlock 后，被锁的应用照样能打开。家长／伴侣 PIN 码可以让关闭变成一个需要认真考虑的决定。

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. WordBlock is Android only for now.

有 iPhone 版吗？

暂时没有，目前只支持安卓。
